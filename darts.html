Step 1: drag to aim, release to lock it in
Two-step throw: drag to aim and release to lock in your target, then pull back and release with the right force — too soft or too hard and the dart lands short or long of your mark. Standard 501: first to exactly 0 wins, but your last dart must land on a double (or the bullseye). Overshoot, land on 1, or reach 0 without a double, and the whole turn is voided.
