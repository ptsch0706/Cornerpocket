Aim
Tap to lock power & drift
Left stick sets where you're aiming — it stays put once you let go, so line it up first. The right dot bounces on its own, both up-down and side-to-side on different cycles — tap it at the moment you want to commit. Dead center and at the top throws true to your aim; off to a side drifts the dart that way, and lower than the top lets gravity drag it down. Standard 501: first to exactly 0 wins, but your last dart must land on a double (or the bullseye). Overshoot, land on 1, or reach 0 without a double, and the whole turn is voided.
